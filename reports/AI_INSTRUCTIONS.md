# AI instruction check

The supplied assignment PDF contains no explicit instruction requiring an AI-use
declaration or AI reporting. It also contains no explicit permission or prohibition
for AI assistance. This finding applies only to the supplied PDF; course policies
or separate instructor instructions were not available.

AI assistance was used to prepare code, documentation, and verification in this
repository. Group members should review and understand the work and follow any
separate course disclosure policy. This statement does not claim independent
student authorship.

The PDF explicitly requires group member names and roll numbers, a single ZIP,
source code, and screenshots of outputs and visualizations. It also requires
comments identifying the eight transformation stages in the Prolog file.

Charts produced by the programs are generated figures. They are not screenshots.
Use the manual capture instructions in `screenshots/README.md` for genuine
screenshots of execution.
